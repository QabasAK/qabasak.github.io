Research areas

  • Adversarial robustness of vision models (KAUST, CyberSaR)
    Implemented FGSM attacks on Faster R-CNN for autonomous-vehicle perception and prototyped a context-consistency defense that flags suspicious detections. Extended the work to vision-language models by adapting Single Image Unlearning (NeurIPS 2024) to repair a backdoored LLaVA-1.5-7B, taking correct probes from 0/10 to 9/10. Supervisor: Prof. Ali Shoker.
  • Hardware security with PUFs
    Capstone on FPGA-based PUF attestation for drone Remote ID modules, followed by open ring-oscillator PUF datasets and a paper on PUF-based memory obfuscation. Supervisor: Dr. Enas Abulibdeh.
  • Biclique enumeration and graph algorithms (PSUT)
    Rebuilt a manuscript on the minimum correct reduction from maximal biclique to maximal clique enumeration, with a GPU construction that scales to 8.5M edges. Also surveyed applications of dominating sets, including driver proteins in protein interaction networks and extractive text summarization. With Dr. Raghda Hriez and Dr. Sahar Al-Eid.
  • Applied mathematics (PSUT)
    Extended Fourier series theory using a gamma-based exponential function and studied accretive matrices. With Dr. Mohammad Sababha.
